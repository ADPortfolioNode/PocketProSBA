# Daily operations — workflows, one task at a time

Daily operations is the service-business page in the app (open it on /daily).
Each tab on that page is a service. Each task below is one job a person does
in that service: what it does, the steps, what you need, and what you get.
Rates and rooms in this document are example placeholders — replace them with
the business's own rates and rooms. Operating hours are 11:00 AM to 11:00 PM
daily. Demo only: these workflows take no payments and make no real
reservations. Verify rates and policies with the business before booking.
SBA guidance on this page is guidance only, not a loan approval — a lender
and the SBA decide eligibility, amount, and timing.

## Task: Check availability — Rooms

Service: Rooms. What it does: see which hours are free for one room on one date.
Steps: 1. Pick a room and a date. 2. Read the day schedule, hour by hour: Free
or Booked. 3. Pick a free start time for the quote and booking tasks.
Needs: Room and date. Result: A free start time inside operating hours.

## Task: Get a quote — Rooms

Service: Rooms. What it does: price a session before anyone commits to it.
Steps: 1. Pick the room, day or evening rate, and number of hours.
2. Choose card or cash — card adds the 4% processing fee.
3. Read the quote: base, fee, total, and 50% deposit due.
Needs: Room, time of day, and hours. Result: A quoted total and deposit, with
nothing booked yet.

## Task: Add a demo booking — Rooms

Service: Rooms. What it does: hold a room for a session in this demo.
Steps: 1. Add a booking name, date, start time, and hours.
2. The page blocks it if the room is already booked for part of that time.
3. The page blocks it if it starts or ends outside operating hours.
Needs: Booking name, date, start time, and hours. Result: The booking appears
in the day schedule and the bookings list.

## Task: Book a recording session — Recording

Service: Recording. What it does: hold the recording studio for a session in
this demo. Steps: 1. Pick a date, start time, and hours inside operating
hours. 2. Add a session name. 3. The page blocks it if the studio is already
booked for part of that time. Needs: Date, start time, hours, and session
name. Result: The session appears in the recording bookings list.

## Task: Confirm engineer and deposit — Recording

Service: Recording. What it does: turn a held session into a confirmed booking
with the studio. Steps: 1. Confirm engineer availability with the studio.
2. Confirm the session minimum: vocal 2 hours, instrumental 3 hours.
3. Take the 50% deposit; the 5-day recording cancellation policy applies.
Needs: Engineer availability and the deposit. Result: A confirmed session,
with its file kept with the booking. Status: Confirm with the studio — this
step is not a form on this page.

## Task: Open a repair intake — Repair

Service: Repair. What it does: log a broken item so the shop can look at it.
Steps: 1. Add the instrument or gear. 2. Describe the issue. 3. Submit the
intake. Needs: Item, and the issue if known. Result: The intake appears in
the repair list, with a free estimate requested.

## Task: Get a free estimate — Repair

Service: Repair. What it does: find out what the repair will cost before any
work starts. Steps: 1. The shop reviews the intake. 2. The shop gives an
estimate. 3. The customer approves it before work begins. Needs: An open
intake. Result: An approved estimate — no work starts without one.
Status: Workflow outline — the estimate step is handled by the shop, not a
form here.

## Task: Rent gear — Rentals & Retail

Service: Rentals & Retail. What it does: lend backline gear to a customer
using the rooms. Steps: 1. Check the gear out to the customer. 2. Check it
back in when it returns. 3. Log any damage or maintenance it needs.
Needs: Gear item and customer. Result: Gear tracked from check-out to
check-in, with damage logged. Status: Workflow outline — no form yet.

## Task: Sell retail — Rentals & Retail

Service: Rentals & Retail. What it does: sell gear and accessories to
customers using the services. Steps: 1. Pick the retail item. 2. Record the
sale to the service customer. 3. Update stock. Needs: Retail item and
customer. Result: A recorded retail sale. Status: Workflow outline — no form
yet.

## Task: Run a community event — Events

Service: Events. What it does: put on an event in the space, such as an Open
Jam, Drum Club, or showcase. Steps: 1. Pick the event type. 2. Book the space
for it on the Rooms tab. 3. Promote it to the community. Needs: Event type
and a booked space. Result: An event with space held for it.

## Task: Support a theatre rehearsal — Events

Service: Events. What it does: host a musical theatre rehearsal as a service
customer. Steps: 1. Book the room for the rehearsal block. 2. Confirm the
schedule with the company. 3. Keep the booking in the day schedule.
Needs: Company, room, and rehearsal dates. Result: A rehearsal block held in
the schedule.

## Task: Discuss growth scope — only if asked — Growth

Service: Growth. What it does: talk through more space, equipment, or
staffing, only when the business asks for it. Steps: 1. Ask whether growth
is wanted at all. 2. Walk the prepared scope: space, soundproofing,
equipment, staffing. 3. Gather lease, build-out, and equipment quotes.
Needs: The business asking for it. Result: A scoped growth discussion, with
quotes to support any financing talk. Status: Prepared scope, only if asked.
Do not assume growth is wanted.

## Task: Explore SBA options — Growth

Service: Growth. What it does: show financing guidance for an existing
business, without promising an outcome. Steps: 1. Ask the concierge about
the growth scope. 2. Compare 7(a), 504, and working-capital guidance.
3. Note what a lender will ask for: financials, tax returns, lease, and
quotes. Needs: A growth scope to discuss. Result: Guidance only — not a
loan approval or eligibility decision. Status: Prepared scope, only if
asked.
